⚙️ How it works

A transparent, fully-offline pipeline — no black boxes, every prediction is explained.

1

👁️ See

Extracts a 512-bin colour signature, texture, symmetry and shape cues, plus a k-means dominant-colour palette.

2

🧪 Sense material

Heuristic vision rules score terracotta, bronze, stone, gold and painted-pottery surface signatures.

3

🔎 Compare

Colour + style similarity ranks every reference artefact; the top-5 vote for period, category and region.

4

🧠 Fuse & explain

Your context (find-spot, material, notes) is fused in, dates are similarity-averaged, and the reasoning is shown.

⚠️ Honest limits: this is an educational stylistic-guessing demo with a small reference library — not a professional authentication or dating service. Real archaeology needs excavation context, lab analysis (TL/OSL, C-14, XRF) and expert eyes. Predictions improve as the library grows (see artifacts_db.json + static/ref/ to add your own).
